# INDUS BRAIN AI

AI-Powered Industrial Knowledge Intelligence Platform

Built for **ET AI Hackathon 2.0**

---

## Problem Statement

PS 8 – AI for Industrial Knowledge Intelligence: Unified Asset & Operations Brain

---

## Overview

INDUS BRAIN AI is an enterprise AI platform that transforms scattered industrial documents into an intelligent, searchable knowledge base.

The platform helps engineers, operators, and maintenance teams quickly retrieve information, visualize knowledge relationships, and improve operational decision-making.

---

## Features

- Smart Document Upload
- Document Intelligence
- Knowledge Graph Visualization
- AI Industrial Copilot
- SOP Knowledge Management
- Interactive Dashboard
- Predictive Maintenance (Prototype)
- Compliance Monitoring (Prototype)
